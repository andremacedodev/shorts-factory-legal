Privacy Policy - Shorts Factory

Last updated: October 2026

Shorts Factory is a personal tool used only by its operator.

Data accessed: after the operator logs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token for the operator's own account. The token is stored only on the operator's own computer and is used only to upload video files as drafts.

The tool does not collect, sell, share or transmit personal data of any other person. It has no analytics, ads or third-party trackers.

The operator can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security & permissions > Apps and services) and can delete the stored token by deleting the local file.
